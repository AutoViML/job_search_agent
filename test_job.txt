Radical AI, Inc. is an artificial intelligence company that is accelerating scientific research & development. We are at the forefront of innovation in the field of materials R&D, a critical driver for advancing our most cutting-edge industries and shaping the future. Breaking away from the traditionally slow and costly R&D process, Radical AI leverages artificial intelligence and machine learning to pioneer generative materials science. This innovative field blends AI, engineering, and materials science, revolutionizing how materials are created and discovered. Radical AI's approach speeds up R&D and addresses global challenges, setting new benchmarks in technology and sustainability.
The opportunityAs a Software Engineer in the Robotics and Automation group at Radical AI, you will design and deploy systems to automate our material science research and discovery laboratory. You will specialize in robotics, automation, and perception software development.
Your experience and vision will shape both the physical and systematic implementation of lab automation. You will collaborate closely with Mechatronics, Mechanical Engineering and Material Scientists to meet rigorous and sometimes dynamic design requirements at a profound deployment speed. Mission
Architect and develop software systems that control and orchestrate robotic workcells for autonomous materials experimentation
Design scalable control frameworks for flexible automation involving robots, motion systems, sensors, and lab instruments
Collaborate with hardware, mechatronics, and science teams to translate experimental workflows into reliable automated processes
Build and maintain APIs and services for scheduling, execution, monitoring, and data capture
Develop simulation, testing, and validation tools to accelerate development and ensure system reliability
Integrate 2D and 3D vision systems (cameras, lighting, depth sensors) with robotic manipulation, motion planning, and execution
Optimize system performance, robustness, and throughput under rapid iteration cycles
Contribute to technical direction, architecture decisions, and best practices across the robotics, perception, and automation software stack
Mentor junior engineers and help establish engineering standards in a fast-growing organization
Bring an attitude of collaboration and open-mindedness, to facilitate fearless and creative problem solving skills that empowers our team to ship world class technology at an unprecedented speed.
