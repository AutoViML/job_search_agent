### 3. Learning Roadmap

Here’s a roadmap for developing the high-priority skills.

---

**Skill: Product Development Lifecycle**
*   **Category:** Domain Knowledge
*   **Frequency:** 3/3
*   **Priority:** Critical
*   **Why it matters:** This is the core process you'll be navigating. Understanding each stage—ideation, design, prototyping, testing, manufacturing, launch, and iteration—is paramount.
*   **Learning Resources:**
    *   **Online Courses:**
        *   Coursera: "New Product Development" by Northwestern University ([https://www.coursera.org/learn/new-product-development](https://www.coursera.org/learn/new-product-development))
        *   Udemy: "Product Development: From Concept to Launch" ([https://www.udemy.com/course/product-development/](https://www.udemy.com/course/product-development/))
    *   **Books:**
        *   "The Lean Startup" by Eric Ries (focuses on iterative development and validating ideas)
        *   "Product Development & Management: Strategy, Planning, and Performance" by Melvin J. Patel
    *   **Projects:** Analyze a product you use daily from its concept to its current iteration. Document its lifecycle stages, identify key challenges, and how they might have been overcome.
*   **Estimated Learning Time:** 1-2 months of focused study and application.
*   **Practical Projects:**
    1.  **Case Study Analysis:** Select a successful or failed product and map out its entire lifecycle, identifying key decisions and outcomes.
    2.  **Personal Project Plan:** Outline the product development lifecycle for a hypothetical product you'd like to design.

**Skill: Cross-functional Collaboration**
*   **Category:** Soft Skill
*   **Frequency:** 3/3
*   **Priority:** Critical
*   **Why it matters:** Product design rarely happens in a vacuum. You'll need to effectively communicate and work with mechanical engineers, electrical engineers, software developers, marketing, and manufacturing teams.
*   **Learning Resources:**
    *   **Online Courses:**
        *   LinkedIn Learning: "Leading with Effective Communication" ([https://www.linkedin.com/learning/paths/lead-with-effective-communication](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/paths/lead-with-effective-communication))
        *   Coursera: "Teamwork Skills: Communicating and Collaborating Effectively in Groups" ([https://www.coursera.org/learn/teamwork-skills](https://www.coursera.org/learn/teamwork-skills))
    *   **Books:**
        *   "Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your Humanity" by Kim Scott (teaches how to give and receive feedback effectively)
        *   "The Five Dysfunctions of a Team" by Patrick Lencioni (understanding team dynamics)
    *   **Projects:** Actively seek opportunities in group projects (academic or extracurricular) to take on roles that require coordinating with others. Practice active listening and clear articulation of ideas.
*   **Estimated Learning Time:** Ongoing; significant improvement through practice and reflection. Initial focused learning: 1 month.
*   **Practical Projects:**
    1.  **ASME Consulting Project Analysis:** Reflect on a past project. Identify specific instances of collaboration, what worked well, and what could be improved. Document these learnings.
    2.  **Simulated Project Handoff:** Create a mock documentation package for a design component, as if handing it off to another discipline (e.g., manufacturing).
